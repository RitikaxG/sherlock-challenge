Project summary:
I built a Candidate Identity Fusion Engine that identifies which live interview participant stream belongs to the candidate. The system fuses participant metadata, meeting events, speech/transcript evidence, LLM-structured transcript role evidence, confidence scoring, safety guardrails, and scenario evaluation.

The solution is designed to stay uncertain when evidence is weak, return ambiguous when streams are too close, and explain why a participant was selected or rejected. The LLM extracts structured transcript evidence only; the deterministic core engine makes the final candidate decision.
